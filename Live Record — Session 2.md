## Conflicts for the DM

Nothing is held outright. These need your call (the same list is in [[WP09 — Session 2]], "As played").

1. **Red-Tusk Rep after the Kettlerock peace (R2-006, R2-007, R2-016).** The files give no number for peace plus 30 rations given openly at the camp. The nearest rows in WP09 §7 are +1 (food in the cache) and +2 (escorting Nakka home). It stands at **−3** (two scouts killed, WP09 §7: −2). Options: **(a) +2, so −1** (recommended: the food was given openly to the band at its camp); (b) +1, so −2; (c) hold at −3 until Nakka is answered. This also swings the branch count: Rep ≥ 0 gives Compact +2, and Rep ≤ −2 gives War +2.
2. **Nakka (WP09 Scene 8).** Her Day 3 Afternoon visit to the bridge was not played. Does Orbal still send her after the peace? If she came, the default (Ondra receives her and turns her away unharmed) gives Compact +1.
3. **Which scout lived** (R2-013, R2-014): Tikk, Brusk or Ozz? WP07 §4.5 says to write it down.
4. **The Day 3 Night raid and Food.** WP09 §7 puts Food at 3 if the stage-2 raid took two sheep. The party made peace that day and the Moorgaunt later died. Options: **(a) Food stays 4** (recommended: no record says a raid happened); (b) Food 3.
5. **The S14 register (R2-009, R2-010).** Did the party claim or correct it, only look, try S15, or take the S11 key? None is recorded. If they corrected it, Instability −2 and Hollow Rep +2 apply.
6. **Shared enemy and a goblin life saved (WP04 §8.3).** Count the Sentinel as a shared enemy both sides know (Compact +2)? Count the spared scout as a goblin life saved (Compact +1)?
7. **Trade 3 → 2 and Reaction 2 → 3.** I applied both from the WP09 §7 rules (the quarry is still shut at the end of Day 3; nobody told Ondra). Confirm or rule otherwise.
