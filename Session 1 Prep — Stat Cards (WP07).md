## What session 1 does NOT need

**Do not print these and do not prepare them.** Nothing in WP08 puts them on the table:

- **The Moorgaunt.** Its rhythm has two nights to run. A party that follows the drag trail on Day 2 finds **the cache, not the animal.**
- **The Listener, the Sentinel.** Stage I has no creature at all.
- **Laughing Hollow wardens.** Stage 1 is Observation. Nobody in Greybank has seen an elf in a generation.
- **Ghesh, Orbal, Threll.** Session 2 and later.
- **The full militia at Defense 3+.** Turnout is 11, and they will not muster tonight.

# THE ONE FIGHT — three Red-Tusk Scouts

**Day 2, Night, at the fold. Fair for five level 3 PCs, and it is not why it is here.**

> **WP04 §6.2 is binding: probes are survivable by both sides. A DM who kills a PC with this has broken canon.**

### RED-TUSK SCOUT ×3

*Small humanoid (goblinoid) · [ADAPTED] · Attitude Hostile at Rep −1*

**AC** 14 · **HP** 10 (3d6) · **Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 (−1) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 8 (−1) |

**Skills** Stealth +7, Survival +3, Perception +3
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., **passive Perception 13**
**Languages** Goblin; one of the three has about six words of Common and one of them is *"hungry"*

**Nimble Escape.** Disengage or Hide as a bonus action, every turn.

**Sent to Look, Not to Fight.** **Withdraws the moment it drops below half hit points**, no morale roll. Getting away is its objective; it has no other.

**Actions**
- **Shortsword.** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) piercing.
- **Shortbow.** +5 to hit, range 80/320. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) piercing.

**Morale.** 1d6 on any trigger. **1** rout · **2** break · **3–4** waver *(break next trigger, no roll)* · **5** hold · **6** harden.
**Triggers:** leader down · half the group down · objective lost · **escape route opened** · overwhelming magic.

### How they behave

**Objective: a hen, a coil of rope, and a count of how many people come running.**

| They will | They will not |
|---|---|
| Come over the wall where the stones are already loose | Kill a human if they can avoid it |
| Shoot if shot at | Enter the cott |
| **Drop everything and run if a way out is open** | Take a child |
| Split up and use Nimble Escape | **Stay** |

### Outcomes

| | State change |
|---|---|
| Let them take it and go | **Red-Tusk Rep −1 → 0.** They report the fold is watched now |
| Drive off, no kills | Nothing. **They come back at the farms next time** |
| Talk to them | **Red-Tusk Rep +1.** Orbal hears about it |
| **Kill any** | **Red-Tusk Rep −2 · War +1.** The survivor reports, and Ghesh counts |
| Capture one | **Compact +1.** Fastest route to Orbal in the campaign |

STR 12 · DEX 10 · CON 12 · INT 10 · WIS 10 · CHA 10

**Untried.** Disadvantage on its **first** attack roll in any combat.
**Knows the Ground.** Ignores difficult terrain from carts, spoil, mud or crowds inside the ring.

- **Billhook.** +3, **reach 10 ft.** *Hit:* 6 (1d10+1) slashing.

### BERRICK HONE — militia captain

*[CAMPAIGN]* · **AC** 15 (jack + shield) · **HP** 26 (4d8+8) · **PP 13**

STR 13 · DEX 14 · CON 14 · INT 11 · WIS 13 · CHA 12

**Rally (1/combat).** Bonus action: every militiaman within 30 ft. that can hear him **ignores the next morale trigger entirely.**

- **Spear.** +4, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) piercing.
- **Shortbow.** +5, 60/240. *Hit:* 6 (1d6+3) piercing.

> **May Break.** If reduced below half HP **while no other militiaman is within 30 ft.**, roll 1d6 privately. **On 1–2 he breaks and runs, and everyone sees it.** Do not roll twice in one session. If he breaks: his attitude to whoever saw drops one step permanently, and **Defense −1**.
