# Session 1 — Items and Rewards

**Status:** Table document. A **partial WP13 slice**, covering only what session 1 can put in the party's hands or in front of their money. Not a work package; WP13 supersedes it and may revise prices.
**Principle (design doc §20):** loot creates strategic choices, not numeric inflation. **Every entry below answers: what are at least three defensible things to do with it?**

---

# 1. The Watch-Glass

**Illefarn · S4, the Watch Chamber · System 2 · [ADAPTED Illefarn work / CAMPAIGN object]**

A shallow basin of dark, still liquid on a low stone table. It lifts out of the table — heavier than it looks, about the weight of a full waterskin, and the liquid does not slop.

> **It has never stopped working.**

## What it does

**Looking into it shows six rings of light in an arc. Three are dark. The dark ones face north-west.**

That is the entire function. It is **information, not power.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Attunement** | **None.** Anyone can look |
| **Charges** | None. It does not run out |
| **Combat use** | **None whatsoever** |
| **Weight** | About 6 lb. Portable, awkward, obviously ancient |
| **Clocks moved by using it** | **None. Nobody notices** — the only system in the site with no faction consequence (WP06 §14) |

**What it is actually for, which the party will not know for two sessions:** it shows **where the ward line runs.** That is the first and always-available solution to the Sentinel — *be outside the line* — handed to a level 3 party for free in session 1.

## The three things to do with it

| Do | Get | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep it** | The ward line, readable, from session 1 to session 4. **The Sentinel's first solution** | Carrying a 6 lb basin of liquid everywhere |
| **Sell it to Malk** | **More coin than exists in Greybank.** See §1.1 | **Blinds the party permanently.** They will never see the line, and in session 4 that is the difference between an ending and a fatality |
| **Give it to the Laughing Hollow** | **Hollow Rep +1**, and Nethra will explain what it shows — which is worth more than the object | The party no longer has it |
| **Leave it where it is** | S4 stays securable if they used the key. They can come back | Somebody else finds it. **If S4 was breached, anything in the site can reach it** |

## 1.1 Malk's offer, in session 1

**If the party mentions it, describes it accurately, or shows it to her, she makes an offer that night.**

> *"Two hundred gold. I have it with me. I won't ask what it does and I won't ask again."*

- **200 gp is not a haggle.** She names one price and lets the silence work.
- **It is comfortably more coin than exists in the whole of Greybank** (≈260 gp, and most of that is not liquid).
- **The Daggerford Commercial clock moves to 2** the moment she has an account of it — **whether or not they sell** (WP04 §7.3).
- **She will not buy anything she cannot authenticate**, and she can authenticate this by looking at it.

> **Do not warn them.** Nobody in session 1 knows what the Watch-Glass is for, including Malk. Selling it is a completely defensible choice made on complete ignorance, which is exactly design doc §20's intent.

# 2. Resonance Key

**Illefarn · S2, in a warden's kit under a bench · [ADAPTED Illefarn work / CAMPAIGN object]**

A palm-sized piece of the same jointless pale stone, cut in a shape that is clearly a shape and not a decoration. **Warm.** Not hot — blood-warm, and it stays that way.

## What it does

**It opens what its span governs.** Not a master key: **each key is tuned to one span of the ward line**, which is why three keys in the site open three different barriers and not one lock.

**This one — the S2 key — opens S4, the Watch Chamber.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Attunement** | None |
| **How you use it** | Hold it near the leaf and it opens. There is no ritual and no check |
| **Other doors it opens** | **In session 1, only S4.** WP13 owns the full mapping |
| **What happens if it is lost** | The Watch Chamber can still be opened by **prising the shutter**, which breaches S4 permanently |

## The three things to do with it

| Do | Get | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep it** | The non-destructive route into S4 now, and into later barriers as they are found | Nothing |
| **Sell it to Malk** | She offers **60 gp**, unprompted, for a piece of worked Illefarn stone she cannot identify | **Every barrier it would have opened must now be broken instead** — and each break is a permanent breach the elves notice |
| **Give it to the Hollow** | **Hollow Rep +1.** Vaerelin takes it without comment | The party has given away their own door |
| **Destroy it** | Nothing. **It is stone and it does not break easily** | — |

## The kit it came in

A warden's kit, complete, **set down under a bench and never collected.** Rations two thousand years gone to dust, a coil of something that was rope, a stone cup, and the key.

**Nothing else in it is worth anything and it should all be described anyway.** Fifteen sets of pegs on the wall above, all empty, and one kit still under the bench.

> **Reputation changes access, not inventory.** The shrine potion stays visibly on the shelf. It is simply not for sale.

## 3.1 The shrine potion argument — Day 2

**After the collapse there is a live argument about whether Dov Marrek is an emergency.**

- **Yorrin Fell** thinks maybe. He has wanted an actual priest for thirty years and this is the sort of thing they were for.
- **Ondra Vell** thinks the harvest is six weeks out and there may be worse coming, and she will not promise something she is not sure she can deliver.

**Let the party into that argument.** It is the best scene in session 1 and nobody has to fight anybody.

**If the party gives Dov one of their own potions instead:** Yorrin +1 attitude, Ondra +1 attitude, **Greybank Reputation +1**, and Maud says *"mm"*, which from her is a great deal.

# 4. What session 1 must not give them

| | Why |
|---|---|
| **Anything with a numeric bonus** | Design doc §20. Utility over numbers, and the Watch-Glass is the model |
| **A second resonance key** | The other two are in S6 and S11 — Stage II and III |
| **Anything the Laughing Hollow would take back by force** | They do that from stage 4. In session 1 they are at 1 or 2 and do nothing |
| **Coin in any quantity** | Greybank has ≈260 gp in total. **Malk is the only real money in the region**, and that is the point |
| **A waystone, or any part of one** | **Never lootable** (WP05, WP06). Three of them are inside the village and a hammer is available |

# 5. Loose ends WP13 must resolve later, and WP08 has not touched

- **Malk's employer.** She was hired by letter, is paid by letter, and has never asked. **WP13 may claim it or leave it; if claimed it must not become a mastermind.**
- **The full resonance key mapping** — which key opens which span.
- **What the workshops (S6) hold.** WP13's chamber.
- **The goblin's bundle in S16**, forty years old, dropped.
- **What the Watch-Glass is actually worth**, against a collector rather than against Greybank. **200 gp is Malk's session-1 offer, not a valuation.**
