# 0. The session in ten lines (proposal)

1. **Opening:** the moor, the dead Moorgaunt, smoke, and **Allino walks out of the heather**. He followed the goblin tracks his own way, and the ranger finds the lost party.
2. **The choice:** walk home now, camp on the moor, or go somewhere else (Kettlerock is only 1 watch away).

**Honest pressure (no railroads).** If the table stalls, push with something **already true** in the world, never with something invented:
- **Hunger:** "Thirty rations for sixty goblins. That was two days ago. Then what?"
- **The cord:** someone touches it in their pack. It's a promise with knots in it.
- **The grind:** if they're underground, the Sentinel's beat gets louder. Ten minutes. Two minutes.
- **Night:** "It's getting dark. On the moor. With no water."
- **A villager asks:** Old Tarn, Rosal or Pell asks the question the players are avoiding.

**Stop points.** If you're out of time, stop at the first of these that's reached, and do §7 (level-up and checklist):
1. **After Nakka's terms** (a decision for next time: "What does Greybank answer?").
2. **At the S14 slot** with the tablet in hand, **before** it goes in.

## 3.1 The moor

> 📜 **Read aloud.** Wind. Nothing but wind. Brown heather to every edge of the world, flat grey sky, and no tree, no wall, no sheep. The thing you killed lies in a smoking heap, all ribs and long grey limbs, and it smells like a burned wet dog the size of a horse. Behind you, the hole you climbed out of breathes cold air. No birds. No hares. No droppings. Nothing lives up here, and now you know why. Your legs are shaking. Someone's stomach growls. Then, far off, someone **whistles**: three notes, a farmer calling a dog. A tall figure is walking toward you through the heather, bow on his back, waving both arms like an idiot.

## 3.2 Allino rejoins

👤 **Allino** · *the superstitious farmer, alone on the uplands for two days*
> **Now:** went his own way. Followed the goblin tracks from the fold north to **Stonecrop Rise (Z7)**, as WP09 Scene 9B planned. Saw the smoke from the burning Moorgaunt and found the lost party. Tired, rattled, unhurt.

**At the start, ask Allino's player one question:** *"Did you follow the tracks all the way to the goblin camp, or stop at the Rise?"*

- **Stopped at the Rise:** he found the goblins' **cache** on the Rise (WP09 Scene 9B) and touched the **cold standing stone, W4**. Nothing else.
- **Went on to the camp:** it's **the same camp** the party visited, **Kettlerock (Z16)**. The Red-Tusk have only one camp; their old home is the Old Delving (Z17). He watched from the rim and didn't go in. **He saw only what anyone on the rim would see** (WP07 E06): hungry people, cold fires, the party's ration sacks. Maybe five goblins coming in with two sheep on Day 4 Morning. No secret talk.

🎲 **DM.** Allino brings **no secret news**. The goblin facts reach the party from the goblins themselves:
- **Ghesh suspects the golem was fake.** He says so at Kettlerock (§5.3) or on his raid night (§6.2): *"The stone-man died too easy. Like smoke."* **(proposed)**

**Allino's ranger skills:** he knows the Rise and the ground back to Greybank. **Advantage on the survey check home** (§3.3).

> 📜 **Read aloud.** You didn't hear him arrive. Nobody did. A tall elf in a grey-green cloak is standing ten paces off, by the stone, as if he grew there. A bow on his back he doesn't reach for. He doesn't shift his weight. He doesn't blink much. He looks at each of you in turn, the way you'd count sheep. Then he speaks, very quietly, and the wind seems to drop so you can hear him.

> 👤 **[Vaerelin Ossuine](#vaerelin-ossuine)** · *Warden-speaker of the Laughing Hollow. Total stillness.*
> **Now:** **Not met.** Neutral, and running out of patience. Watched them open the north gate. **Will speak once and never again.**

**What he says, once (proposed wording; WP04 §5.5 + the open gate):**

> *"You opened the north door. You carried things out of the stone, and sold them. Do not open it further. Do not carry anything out. What you put into the stone, leave in the stone. We have already begun to take back what you have taken. We will go on, from wherever it is. I will not say this again."*

Then questions. **He answers only what's asked, in as few words as possible. Never why.**
