**What's going on (DM)**

- **The Sentinel's circuit** is S9 → S12 → S9, about **one watch** round trip. It turns on the S9 floor **once per watch, at the half-watch mark** (WP09 §2.5). **Deterministic. Never roll for where it is.**

- **The S13 branch** is about **30 minutes** north of S9. So the Sentinel passes the branch about **30 min after it leaves S9 going north**, and again about **30 min before it gets back**. *(Session 2 timings, events S02-E0022–E0023.)*
- **Presence-sense 120 ft.** It doesn't need to see you. Invisibility, illusions and disguises do nothing.
- **The safe plan:** wait at S9 for the turn (the grind gets loud: 10 min, then 2 min, then the floor rotates). Let it go north. **Count to a hundred** (stay over 120 ft behind). Follow. Turn left into S13. You have **about three hours** before it comes back past the branch.
