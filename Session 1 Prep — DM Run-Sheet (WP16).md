- **Nobody suggests they go into the aperture.** Available and attractive, never assigned.
- **No villager will enter the site, ever**, for any inducement.
- **The militia never crosses the boundary stones.**
- **Probes are survivable by both sides.** Do not kill a PC with three scouts.
- **Do not escalate to force engagement.** A do-nothing session ends at Reaction 2 and that is fine.
- **Say the number of days out loud** every time a project is mentioned.
