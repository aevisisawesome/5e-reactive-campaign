# Previously, in Greybank…

*Session 1 recap for the players. No secrets inside, only what your characters saw (and a few jokes at their expense).*

Four strangers walked into a quiet river village on the road to Daggerford. A hooded cat in half plate (**Howling Matza**). A halfling who is *definitely* in a famous boyband and *definitely* will tell you what the D stands for, just never the same answer twice (**Bar.D.Sync**). A superstitious ex-potato farmer with a very good bow (**Allino**). And seven and a half feet of goliath who drinks other people's beer (**Biggy Smalls**). Old Tarn on his bench told them to move along. One whistle tune, one song and one gold piece later, he is president of the fan club. At the Sundered Cart the bards sang for their beds, Biggy drank Vadd the carter's beer (Vadd remembers), Bar.D.Sync sold the village's biggest secret to Serina Malk from Daggerford for five gold, and the foreman Durrow quietly told Howling Matza he'd been inside the quarry's broken wall. Then a terrified halfling, Nissa, burst through the door: voices at the sheep pen. Small ones. *Then something big.* You believed her. You went out in the rain. Respect.

At the fold you found a dog frozen in fear and **two** problems for the price of one: a spot where something large had broken over the top of the wall, and, some way off, stones pulled out and badly put back, with goblin tracks all around. Next morning the quarry cracked open with a sound like the world clearing its throat. A man, Dov Marrek, was pinned under a slab. You *walked* there (the DM noticed). Biggy and Allino lifted the slab with a dozen quarrymen, and Howling Matza's magic saved Dov's life, though not his leg. Howling Matza then told the whole crew about the goblin tracks, so news now travels at the speed of a tabaxi with an audience.

Then you went down. Under the quarry: elven ruins, thousands of years old. A ramp of seamless pale stone. A hall of carved Elvish names, the last ones cut in a hurry (never a good sign). A locked room, opened with a *Knock* spell, holding a basin of dark liquid with six rings of light: three lit, three dark. Workshops, and racks of stone tablets "like books in a library". A rectangular key that fits a socket made of the same stone as the old tower in the village: click, flash, doors unlock, and a **fourth ring** lights up. Allino pulled it out to test it. Things went dark. He put it back. Classic adventurer logic: if the glowing thing works, leave it in. Further down, a flooded stair with eleven old bodies on the dry steps, and something large, slimy and blind in the black water. Biggy lassoed a corpse out like a cowboy who went fishing, the thing came for it and then turned back, and on the body you found an old harness, a stone cup and a **second key**. That key opened a huge door onto a dead-straight gallery, its floor worn an inch deep by footsteps. *Whose* footsteps? You decided not to find out today. Wisdom check: passed.

Back in town, you sold about thirty tablets to Serina at 10 gp each (75 gp apiece, thank you very much). She's sending them off to be translated: "a few days". You told two militiamen to keep people out past the big hall, and you asked around about the old tower. Nobody knows much, which has never stopped Biggy, who now plans to build beds and sleep in it. So: Evening of Day 2. Coin in your pockets, a blind thing under the quarry, goblin tracks at the fold, something big still out there, a maimed quarryman, a charcoal delivery that's mysteriously late, a Reeve who will want a word, and a promise to Durrow. Sleep well. What could possibly go wrong?
