Part 1

The Campaign in Brief

1.1 The pitch · DM only

the pitch

A broke little quarry village sits on top of an ancient fence that has been failing for two thousand years. Elves want it sealed. Goblin refugees are starving at the door. A city trader wants to buy the pieces. And a stone guard down below still thinks everyone is an intruder. The heroes didn't come to save anyone. They came to cross the bridge.

  • Game: D&D 5e, Forgotten Realms, near Daggerford. Lore setting, CAMPAIGN story
  • Length: 4 sessions, plus an optional 5th. Levels: start at 3, finish around 5–6. The party is level 4 from Session 2.
  • The table: four players, friends playing for fun. See the player characters.

1.2 How this campaign plays

This is a reactive sandbox: a small world that changes because of what the players do, and gets worse when they do nothing. Full version: What kind of campaign this is and the five rules.

Rule What it means at the table
The world doesn't scale to the partySome things are too strong. Running away is a real, smart choice
Danger is always warned firstEvery deadly thing is telegraphed (four levels, from a hint to "it's right there"). No surprise party-killers. See the fairness contract
Consequences stickDead stays dead, broken stays broken, used items are gone. Nothing resets between sessions
No quest-giversProblems sit on the table and get worse if ignored. Nobody hands out missions
No villainEvery faction wants something reasonable. They clash anyway
Combat is one answer of manyEvery hard problem has a non-fighting way through: talk, sneak, trade, seal, run
DM

Two standing habits of this DM: when a choice is between a harsh, permanent consequence and a soft, recoverable one, take the harsh one and telegraph it well. And one deliberate kindness: a span lit by a key counts as working for the ending (ruling A2).

1.3 What the story is about, and how it can end · DM only

The players don't know the "story" yet. They will find it by exploring. The campaign is about one question: who will hold the fence, and at what price?

There is no fixed ending. It comes from their choices (where it can go):

  • Fortified village · village abandoned or destroyed · goblin alliance · elven protectorate · merchant boom · ruins disaster · party outlawed · or, most likely, a messy mix.

The best ending is expensive and needs teamwork: at least 3 living spans of the fence (they have 4 right now), the elves on side, the anchor done right (done), the stone guard "re-programmed" instead of fought, and a full watch at the fence's heart to re-power it.

If the party does nothing, Greybank loses, slowly and in plain sight, having warned everyone. See the default.

1.4 The place · DM only

  • Greybank has 143 people, one bridge and a white-stone quarry. It is poor: the whole village holds about 260 gp. No wall, just an old earth ring with four gaps. One bell, which doesn't reach the quarry, the fold or the far bank. See Greybank.
  • Daggerford is about 2 days south-east. That's where the money is.
  • The region has 18 zones: moors, woods, a goblin camp at Kettlerock, an elf wood at the Hollowmarch. See the region.
  • Time moves in 4-hour watches: Dawn, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, Deep Night. A long rest is 2 watches.

1.6 The secret under the quarry · DM only

Campaign, on ADAPTED Illefarn foundations Full version: The Marrowward.

  • The quarry has cut into the Marrowward: an ancient elven fence across the land, built to keep things out of the region.
  • Six waystones (fence posts) stand in a chain. Between each pair runs an invisible span, powered from the Ward Heart deep underground, like a fuse box. Three posts are hidden inside Greybank: the tower base (W1), the shrine foundation (W2) and the graveyard wall (W3). Nobody knows.
  • Dead posts: W4 and W5. W3 is dead too, but key A (in the anchor socket) lights it, so 4 of 6 count right now. Pull the key and W3 goes dark. See the six posts.
  • Walking across the line does nothing. It matters only to monsters coming in, and to the Sentinel.
  • Breaking a post kills its span forever. Two broken and the good ending is gone.
  • The Sentinel is the fence's stone guard. Its map is broken, so it treats everyone inside the line as an intruder. It can't be beaten in a fight. It can be avoided, sealed off, or eventually re-programmed. See the Sentinel.
  • The Listener is a blind thing in the underground water. It hunts by feeling footsteps on stone. Standing in running water hides you.
  • The goblins were driven out of their old home by the Sentinel. Without knowing it, they've been blocking the fence's back door. See the Red-Tusk.
  • The Moorgaunt, a starving moor predator, is what drags sheep from the fold. See the Moorgaunt.
  • Nobody knows the whole truth. Not the elves, not the goblins, not the traders. The players have to put it together. See who believes what.

Part 2

Session 1, Side by Side

Played 29 Aug 2026. In-world: Day 1 Afternoon → Day 2 Evening. Left column: what the DM knows happened. Right column: what the players saw, as told in their recap. Read across each row to see what the players know, and what they don't yet.

2.1 Day 1 · Afternoon: arrival

The party came from the north (Waterdeep, heading for Daggerford), so they reached the village before the bridge. Hook 1: just passing through. The DM opened with the fairness contract: running is legitimate, nothing scales, consequences stick. Old Tarn was hostile to the hooded cat until the two musicians charmed him. Howling Matza gave him 1 gp to pass on news. He's now a fan and tells everyone (and it's "a fistful" by now).

What the players saw

A river with one bridge, stone-and-thatch cottages inside an old earth ring, a green with a well and a bell post, and a white quarry on the hill. An old man on a bench told them to move along, until a whistle tune and a song changed his mind. He grumbled that Daggerford took the village's trade and pointed them to the inn. Howling Matza gave him a gold piece.

2.2 Day 1 · Evening: the Sundered Cart

  • The deal: Bar.D.Sync and Howling Matza played for free beds. Above decent. Honoured, plus free milk next morning. Rosal is Friendly.
  • Biggy drank Vadd's beer. Stare-off. Vadd is Wary toward Biggy. Vadd claims no charcoal order. Truth: the charcoal is four days late because of him.
  • Serina Malk pays 5 gp per lead. Bar.D.Sync sold her the true lead that the miners hit ancient construction. That started the Daggerford clock.
  • Durrow's secret, to Howling Matza alone: he went in "last night", "a large room", felt the air move. Truth: he went ten feet. He gave permission to investigate, and the party promised him a share.
  • A frightened halfling (Nissa Crake) reported voices and something big at the fold. The party believed her and went. That makes Nissa Friendly for good.
What the players saw

Rosal Tunn's inn, four straw pallets at 5 coppers. A good show. Six quarrymen sitting quiet; one admitted the stone "comes out wrong, like it was placed there by someone." The dust-white foreman let them investigate, and told Howling Matza more privately. Serina, a composed woman from Daggerford, pays 5 gp a lead and wants first look at anything found. The charcoal comes from a camp across the river; Vadd hauls it, and wasn't pleased when Biggy drank his beer. Late at night, a frightened halfling burst in: voices near the sheep pen, small ones, then something big.

2.3 Day 1 · Night: the fold

The party found both halves of the two-cause puzzle on the first night: Howling Matza found the wall top broken over by something big; Allino found stones pulled out and put back badly, with clear goblin tracks. The DM said the two signs are separate. They didn't track in the dark. Truth: the Moorgaunt takes sheep; the goblin scouts take small things. Bessa wasn't there.

What the players saw

A dog frozen, staring at the dark. Two things wrong with the wall, some way apart: one spot broken over the top by something large; another where stones had been pulled out and clumsily put back, with goblin tracks all around. Too dark and wet to follow. Back to bed.

2.4 Day 2 · Morning: the quarry collapse

The collapse fired on schedule. The party walked instead of running, so Dov Marrek was pinned for too long. Biggy and Allino heaved the slab off with the crew. Maud arrived; Howling Matza's Cure Wounds saved his life, but not the leg. No healer's kit was used (still 2). Greybank Rep +1. Howling Matza told the whole crew about the goblin tracks, so the goblin threat is now public, and the party is the source. Durrow went to tell the Reeve.

What the players saw

A long grinding crack and dust over the quarry. A runner: they'd cut into a wall "thousands of years old" and it came down. A man pinned under a huge slab. Biggy, Allino and a dozen men lifted it, too late for the leg. Maud the herbwife arrived; Howling Matza's magic closed the wound. Maud said the village has only two healer's kits and the shepherd just lost a fourth sheep in two weeks. The quarry shut for the day.

2.5 Day 2 · late Morning: under the quarry, Stage I

About a watch and a half underground. See Stage I in one visit.

  • S1 the ramp: one set of bootprints turning back at ten feet (Durrow's).
  • S2 the hall: an Elvish roster of wardens, the last names cut in a hurry. Not found: a warden's kit under a bench.
  • S4: opened with Knock. The Watch-Glass: six rings, three lit. Truth: it maps the fence; the dark ones face north-west.
  • S3: a rectangular socket, the same stone as the tower (Howling Matza spotted it).
What the players saw

A broad ramp of seamless pale stone. A great empty hall with Elvish names carved on the far wall, the last ones cut in a hurry. A locked chamber, opened with a knock spell: a basin of still dark liquid with six rings of light, three lit and three dark. A round room with a worn rectangular socket, the same pale stone as the old tower in the village.

2.6 Day 2 · Afternoon: the workshops and the key

  • S6 the workshops: racks of stone tablets "like books in a library". They took tablets, tools and trinkets (Bar.D.Sync, Biggy) and key A. Truth: the records explain the Sentinel, the Deterrent Chain and the passwords for the deep site.
  • Key A into the S3 socket: a light, doors released, the Watch-Glass now shows 4 of 6. Allino tested it: pull the key and the door relocks and the ring goes dark. They left it in. Truth: that's the Ward Anchor turned on, lighting W3. The elves felt it (Hollow clock 3, Hollow Rep +1). It also keeps the Moorgaunt away.
What the players saw

Workshops: stone and metal shops, stores, and a room of carved stone tablets. They took tablets and a rectangular key. In the socket, the key made a light flash and unlocked doors, and the basin then showed four rings lit. Pull the key, and the door relocks.

2.7 Day 2 · Afternoon: the Drowned Stair and the gallery

  • S8: stairs into black water, eleven bodies on the dry steps (nine goblins, two ancient elf wardens). A step on dry stone woke the Listener. Biggy lassoed a warden's body out; the thing came after it and stopped. Allino saw it is blind. The DM said plainly: stone sets it off, water doesn't.
  • On the warden: a harness, the stone cup, and key B.
  • Key B opened the big stone leaf (S9). Beyond: the Long Gallery with an inch-deep groove worn by footsteps. They looked and turned back. Truth: the Sentinel walks there. They left key B in the lock, so the door is still open.
What the players saw

A flooded stair with the long dead on the dry steps: nine goblins and two figures in ancient elven armour. Something large moved in the black water when Howling Matza stepped on the stone. Biggy hauled a body clear; a big, slimy, blind creature heaved out after it, then turned back. It ignored a stone thrown into the water. On the body: an old harness, a stone cup and a second key. The second key opened a huge door onto a dead-straight gallery, its floor worn an inch deep by footsteps. They decided not to find out what walks there, yet.

2.8 Day 2 · Evening: back in town

  • They warned two miner-militiamen to keep everyone out past the big hall.
  • They sold about 30 tablets to Serina at 10 gp each (about 300 gp, 75 each) and told her about the blind creature. Truth: Daggerford clock → 3; translations reach the city in a few days. She refused the stone cup.
  • Nobody they asked knows much about the tower. Biggy wants to build beds and sleep in it.
  • Players' plans: everyone sleeps, then goes to the tower. Then the Reeve (Howling Matza), the charcoal man and the footprints (Bar.D.Sync), the ruins (Allino), the goblin trail and the ruins (Biggy).
What the players saw

Serina bought the tablets (about thirty) at 10 gp apiece and is sending them off to be translated: "a few days". She wasn't interested in the stone cup. Nobody seems to know much about the old tower. Evening of Day 2, at the Sundered Cart: coin in pocket, a blind thing under the quarry, goblin tracks at the fold, a maimed quarryman, an unanswered charcoal question, and a Reeve who will want to hear from them.

Session 2, Side by Side

Played 1 Nov 2026. In-world: Day 2 Evening → Day 4 (the later times are inferred). Left column: what the DM knows happened. Right column: what the players saw. The party was away from Greybank for all of Day 3, so most of the village's day happened without them. Full detail: As played.

3.1 Day 2 · Evening and Night: the Cart and the fold raid

  • Session 2 opened at the Sundered Cart. The fold raid fired that night (three Red-Tusk scouts: Tikk, Brusk and Ozz).
  • The party fought it. They killed two scouts and incapacitated the third, and carried him up to the Cart.
  • Truth: killing scouts costs Red-Tusk Rep −2 (it is now −3) and adds War +1. Cart NPCs saw them carry him in, so someone will tell the Reeve. The party does not know that. They have not met her yet.
What the players saw

A night of dogs and shadows at the fold. Three small, quick goblins in the byre door. Two were killed, and one was knocked out and carried back to a pallet at the Cart, with half the common room watching.

3.2 Day 3 · Morning: the chase

  • The wounded scout got away. He fled, and the party chased him for about three hours and reached Kettlerock, the band's current camp. (The DM overruled the usual three-watch distance.)
  • They left Greybank on Day 3 Morning. Because of this they missed Bessa's Dawn report, Threll's clerk, Nakka at the bridge and Vaerelin's warning.
  • Truth: Ghesh now knows their number and reach. Which scout got away is not yet recorded.
What the players saw

The prisoner slipped away wounded. They followed his trail north, hour after hour, over the moor.

3.3 Day 3 · Kettlerock: the talk, the chaos and the peace

  • Ghesh came out and talked. He told them the story of the Sentinel. He told them nothing about what lies behind the Old Delving's door.
  • It went to chaos. Aldracu tried to intimidate the whole camp and failed. Bar.D.Sync cast a stone golem illusion that scared off the camp's attack. The party ran into the bushes.
  • They staged a kill of the illusion to win the goblins' trust. Then they made peace and gave the band 30 rations.
  • Orbal accepted the food, and the party's promise to defeat the Sentinel.
  • Truth: the golem was an illusion. The band believes it was real. If Ghesh learns otherwise, the trust breaks. The count so far favours the Compact branch.
What the players saw

A starving camp in a bowl of broken rock. A thin, scarred war-leader who said the important part twice. He told them of something that came at night and did not hurry. A shouting match, a huge stone shape that scared off the attack, and a wild run into the scrub. Then a truce, and thirty rations passed across. An old goblin with a bundle of knotted cords took the food, and the party's word that they would deal with the stone thing.

3.4 Day 3 · evening: the Old Delving and the Kettlerock Gate

  • The party went on to the Old Delving, the band's old home, and cleared the rubble from the doorway.
  • The Kettlerock Gate (S12) is open for good. (Truth: the goblins had piled it shut out of terror, without knowing what it held back. At Instability 1 the Sentinel still stays in the tunnels. The Hollow notices openings: their clock is now 4.)
What the players saw

A worked cleft in the hills, a door of pale stone piled shut with rock and timber that someone had kept up by hand. They dug it out.

3.5 Day 3 Night to Day 4: the north end of the Marrowward

  • They rested outside S12, then spent time inside and came out after another half day.
  • They sneaked past the Sentinel in the Long Gallery again. Howling Matza's Knock opened the door at the Approach of Names (S13). Beyond it is S14, with the authorisation register.
  • Ruling: the Sentinel walks only from S9 to S12. It is not in S13–S16. What the party did at the register is not recorded.
What the players saw

The long straight gallery, the thing that walks it, and the corridor of cut names beyond it. A door that would not take a name opened to a spell.

3.6 Day 4 · Hagsfoot Moor: the Moorgaunt

  • The party came out onto Hagsfoot Moor and killed the Moorgaunt at the exit. They want to carry its body back to Greybank.
  • Truth: the hole in the fence is still open. The beast was a symptom. Z4 and Z11 are Stage 1.
What the players saw

A thin grey animal with every rib showing, at the mouth of a rubble slope. It dragged, it bit, and it died on the moor. The slope behind it still leads down.

Handover: Running Session 3

If you are taking over as DM, this is your checklist. The full book is Session Two with its As played part.

4.1 First ten minutes at the table · DM only

  1. Say where they are: Hagsfoot Moor, the Moorgaunt dead behind them, the open slope at their backs, and the Red-Tusk fed and at peace.
  2. Ask what is not recorded (see the open questions in As played): which scout got away, what they did at the register, whether Nakka still came.
  3. Decide what Greybank did without them on Day 3 and Day 4: Bessa, Threll's clerk, Serina's departure, the Day 4 moot.
  4. Ask each player: "What does your character intend to do next?"

4.2 The things a new DM must not miss · DM only

  1. The party has been away since Day 3 Morning. Greybank ran without them. The Reeve has heard about the goblin at the Cart and will ask.
  2. The Red-Tusk branch is not decided. Compact leads on what is certain. Red-Tusk Rep (−3) must be rescored for the peace. The staged golem kill is a lie.
  3. The promise to Orbal: defeat the Sentinel. It cannot be killed. System 6 corrected in S14 is the answer.
  4. The Kettlerock Gate (S12) is open for good. The Hollow is at stage 4: something the party relies on will quietly go missing.
  5. The hole in the fence (S16) is still open. Killing the Moorgaunt did not close it. Say so out loud.
  6. The Sentinel walks only S9 to S12. S13–S16 have no Sentinel (ruling R2-010).
  7. Key A must stay in the socket. Key B is in the S9 lock.
  8. Every sale of an Illefarn object pushes the elves' and Daggerford's clocks.
  9. Durrow is owed 30 gp.
  10. At the end, run the checklist and ask each player what their character intends next. End-of-session checklist.
