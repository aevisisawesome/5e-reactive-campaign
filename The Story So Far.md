# 1. The Campaign in Brief

## 1.1 The pitch · DM only

> 📜 **Read aloud (the pitch).** A broke little quarry village sits on top of an ancient fence that has been failing for two thousand years. Elves want it sealed. Goblin refugees are starving at the door. A city trader wants to buy the pieces. And a stone guard down below still thinks everyone is an intruder. The heroes didn't come to save anyone. They came to cross the bridge.

- **Game:** D&D 5e, Forgotten Realms, near Daggerford. **[LORE setting, CAMPAIGN story]**
- **Length:** 4 sessions, plus an optional 5th. **Levels:** start at 3, finish around 5–6. **The party is level 4 from Session 2.**
- **The table:** four players, friends playing for fun. See [the player characters](#34-the-player-characters).

## 1.2 How this campaign plays

This is a **reactive sandbox**: a small world that changes because of what the players do, and gets worse when they do nothing. Full version: [What kind of campaign this is](01-introduction.html#h-what-this-is) and [the five rules](01-introduction.html#h-five-rules).

| Rule | What it means at the table |
|---|---|
| **The world doesn't scale to the party** | Some things are too strong. Running away is a real, smart choice |
| **Danger is always warned first** | Every deadly thing is telegraphed (four levels, from a hint to "it's right there"). No surprise party-killers. See [the fairness contract](01-introduction.html#h-fairness) |
| **Consequences stick** | Dead stays dead, broken stays broken, used items are gone. Nothing resets between sessions |
| **No quest-givers** | Problems sit on the table and **get worse if ignored**. Nobody hands out missions |
| **No villain** | Every faction wants something reasonable. They clash anyway |
| **Combat is one answer of many** | Every hard problem has a non-fighting way through: talk, sneak, trade, seal, run |

> 🎲 **DM.** Two standing habits of this DM: **when a choice is between a harsh, permanent consequence and a soft, recoverable one, take the harsh one** and telegraph it well. And one deliberate kindness: **a span lit by a key counts as working** for the ending (ruling A2).

## 1.3 What the story is about, and how it can end · DM only

The players don't know the "story" yet. They will find it by exploring. The campaign is about **one question: who will hold the fence, and at what price?**

There is **no fixed ending**. It comes from their choices ([where it can go](02-background.html#h-where)):

- **Fortified village** · **village abandoned or destroyed** · **goblin alliance** · **elven protectorate** · **merchant boom** · **ruins disaster** · **party outlawed** · or, most likely, **a messy mix**.

**The best ending is expensive and needs teamwork:** at least 3 living spans of the fence (they have 4 right now), the elves on side, the anchor done right (done), the stone guard "re-programmed" instead of fought, and a full watch at the fence's heart to re-power it.

**If the party does nothing, Greybank loses**, slowly and in plain sight, having warned everyone. See [the default](02-background.html#h-default).

## 1.4 The place · DM only

- **Greybank** has 143 people, one bridge and a white-stone quarry. It is poor: the whole village holds about 260 gp. No wall, just an old earth ring with four gaps. One bell, which doesn't reach the quarry, the fold or the far bank. See [Greybank](01-introduction.html#h-greybank).
- **Daggerford** is about 2 days south-east. That's where the money is.
- **The region** has 18 zones: moors, woods, a goblin camp at Kettlerock, an elf wood at the Hollowmarch. See [the region](01-introduction.html#h-the-region).
- **Time** moves in **4-hour watches**: Dawn, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, Deep Night. A long rest is 2 watches.

## 1.6 The secret under the quarry · DM only

**[CAMPAIGN, on ADAPTED Illefarn foundations]** Full version: [The Marrowward](02-background.html#h-marrowward).

- The quarry has cut into the **Marrowward**: an ancient elven **fence across the land**, built to keep things out of the region.
- **Six waystones** (fence posts) stand in a chain. Between each pair runs an invisible span, powered from the **Ward Heart** deep underground, like a fuse box. **Three posts are hidden inside Greybank:** the tower base (W1), the shrine foundation (W2) and the graveyard wall (W3). Nobody knows.
- **Dead posts: W4 and W5. W3 is dead too, but key A (in the anchor socket) lights it**, so **4 of 6 count** right now. Pull the key and W3 goes dark. See [the six posts](07-session-two.html#s2-26-the-ward-line-the-six-posts).
- **Walking across the line does nothing.** It matters only to monsters coming in, and to the Sentinel.
- **Breaking a post** kills its span forever. **Two broken and the good ending is gone.**
- **The Sentinel** is the fence's stone guard. Its map is broken, so it treats **everyone** inside the line as an intruder. **It can't be beaten in a fight.** It can be avoided, sealed off, or eventually re-programmed. See [the Sentinel](02-background.html#h-sentinel).
- **The Listener** is a blind thing in the underground water. It hunts by feeling footsteps on stone. **Standing in running water hides you.**
- **The goblins** were driven out of their old home by the Sentinel. Without knowing it, they've been blocking the fence's back door. See [the Red-Tusk](02-background.html#h-red-tusk).
- **The Moorgaunt**, a starving moor predator, is what drags sheep from the fold. See [the Moorgaunt](02-background.html#h-moorgaunt).
- **Nobody knows the whole truth.** Not the elves, not the goblins, not the traders. The players have to put it together. See [who believes what](02-background.html#h-believes).

# 2. Session 1, Side by Side

**Played 29 Aug 2026. In-world: Day 1 Afternoon → Day 2 Evening.** Left column: **what the DM knows** happened. Right column: **what the players saw**, as told in their recap. Read across each row to see what the players know, and what they don't yet.

## 2.1 Day 1 · Afternoon: arrival

The party came **from the north** (Waterdeep, heading for Daggerford), so they reached the village before the bridge. **Hook 1: just passing through.** The DM opened with the fairness contract: running is legitimate, nothing scales, consequences stick. **Old Tarn** was hostile to the hooded cat until the two musicians charmed him. **Howling Matza gave him 1 gp** to pass on news. He's now a fan and tells everyone (and it's "a fistful" by now).

> 🎭 **What the players saw.** A river with one bridge, stone-and-thatch cottages inside an old earth ring, a green with a well and a bell post, and a white quarry on the hill. An old man on a bench told them to move along, until a whistle tune and a song changed his mind. He grumbled that Daggerford took the village's trade and pointed them to the inn. Howling Matza gave him a gold piece.

## 2.2 Day 1 · Evening: the Sundered Cart

- **The deal:** Bar.D.Sync and Howling Matza played for free beds. Above decent. Honoured, plus free milk next morning. **Rosal is Friendly.**
- **Biggy drank Vadd's beer.** Stare-off. **Vadd is Wary toward Biggy.** Vadd claims no charcoal order. *Truth: the charcoal is four days late because of him.*
- **Serina Malk** pays **5 gp per lead**. **Bar.D.Sync sold her the true lead** that the miners hit ancient construction. That started the Daggerford clock.
- **Durrow's secret, to Howling Matza alone:** he went in "last night", "a large room", felt the air move. *Truth: he went ten feet.* He gave permission to investigate, and **the party promised him a share**.
- **A frightened halfling (Nissa Crake)** reported voices and something big at the fold. **The party believed her and went.** That makes Nissa Friendly for good.

> 🎭 **What the players saw.** Rosal Tunn's inn, four straw pallets at 5 coppers. A good show. Six quarrymen sitting quiet; one admitted the stone "comes out wrong, like it was placed there by someone." The dust-white foreman let them investigate, and told Howling Matza more privately. Serina, a composed woman from Daggerford, pays 5 gp a lead and wants first look at anything found. The charcoal comes from a camp across the river; Vadd hauls it, and wasn't pleased when Biggy drank his beer. Late at night, a frightened halfling burst in: voices near the sheep pen, small ones, then something big.

## 2.3 Day 1 · Night: the fold

The party found **both halves of the two-cause puzzle on the first night**: Howling Matza found the wall top broken over by something big; **Allino found stones pulled out and put back badly, with clear goblin tracks**. The DM said the two signs are separate. They didn't track in the dark. *Truth: the Moorgaunt takes sheep; the goblin scouts take small things.* Bessa wasn't there.

> 🎭 **What the players saw.** A dog frozen, staring at the dark. Two things wrong with the wall, some way apart: one spot broken over the top by something large; another where stones had been pulled out and clumsily put back, with goblin tracks all around. Too dark and wet to follow. Back to bed.

## 2.4 Day 2 · Morning: the quarry collapse

The collapse fired on schedule. **The party walked** instead of running, so **Dov Marrek** was pinned for too long. **Biggy and Allino** heaved the slab off with the crew. **Maud arrived; Howling Matza's Cure Wounds saved his life, but not the leg.** No healer's kit was used (still 2). **Greybank Rep +1.** Howling Matza **told the whole crew about the goblin tracks**, so the goblin threat is now public, and the party is the source. Durrow went to tell the Reeve.

> 🎭 **What the players saw.** A long grinding crack and dust over the quarry. A runner: they'd cut into a wall "thousands of years old" and it came down. A man pinned under a huge slab. Biggy, Allino and a dozen men lifted it, too late for the leg. Maud the herbwife arrived; Howling Matza's magic closed the wound. Maud said the village has only two healer's kits and the shepherd just lost a fourth sheep in two weeks. The quarry shut for the day.

## 2.5 Day 2 · late Morning: under the quarry, Stage I

About a watch and a half underground. See [Stage I in one visit](03-session-one.html#h-stage-one).

- **S1 the ramp:** one set of bootprints turning back at ten feet (Durrow's).
- **S2 the hall:** an Elvish **roster of wardens**, the last names cut in a hurry. *Not found: a warden's kit under a bench.*
- **S4:** opened with **Knock**. **The Watch-Glass:** six rings, three lit. *Truth: it maps the fence; the dark ones face north-west.*
- **S3:** a rectangular socket, **the same stone as the tower** (Howling Matza spotted it).

> 🎭 **What the players saw.** A broad ramp of seamless pale stone. A great empty hall with Elvish names carved on the far wall, the last ones cut in a hurry. A locked chamber, opened with a knock spell: a basin of still dark liquid with six rings of light, three lit and three dark. A round room with a worn rectangular socket, the same pale stone as the old tower in the village.

## 2.6 Day 2 · Afternoon: the workshops and the key

- **S6 the workshops:** racks of stone tablets "like books in a library". They took **tablets**, **tools and trinkets** (Bar.D.Sync, Biggy) and **key A**. *Truth: the records explain the Sentinel, the Deterrent Chain and the passwords for the deep site.*
- **Key A into the S3 socket:** a light, doors released, **the Watch-Glass now shows 4 of 6**. Allino tested it: pull the key and the door relocks and the ring goes dark. **They left it in.** *Truth: that's the Ward Anchor turned on, lighting W3. The elves felt it (Hollow clock 3, Hollow Rep +1). It also keeps the Moorgaunt away.*

> 🎭 **What the players saw.** Workshops: stone and metal shops, stores, and a room of carved stone tablets. They took tablets and a rectangular key. In the socket, the key made a light flash and unlocked doors, and the basin then showed four rings lit. Pull the key, and the door relocks.

## 2.7 Day 2 · Afternoon: the Drowned Stair and the gallery

- **S8:** stairs into black water, **eleven bodies on the dry steps** (nine goblins, two ancient elf wardens). A step on dry stone woke **the Listener**. **Biggy lassoed a warden's body** out; the thing came after it and stopped. **Allino saw it is blind.** The DM said plainly: stone sets it off, water doesn't.
- **On the warden:** a harness, **the stone cup**, and **key B**.
- **Key B** opened the big stone leaf (S9). Beyond: **the Long Gallery** with an **inch-deep groove** worn by footsteps. They looked and turned back. *Truth: the Sentinel walks there. They left key B in the lock, so the door is still open.*

> 🎭 **What the players saw.** A flooded stair with the long dead on the dry steps: nine goblins and two figures in ancient elven armour. Something large moved in the black water when Howling Matza stepped on the stone. Biggy hauled a body clear; a big, slimy, blind creature heaved out after it, then turned back. It ignored a stone thrown into the water. On the body: an old harness, a stone cup and a second key. The second key opened a huge door onto a dead-straight gallery, its floor worn an inch deep by footsteps. They decided not to find out what walks there, yet.

## 2.8 Day 2 · Evening: back in town

- They warned two miner-militiamen to keep everyone out past the big hall.
- **They sold about 30 tablets to Serina at 10 gp each (about 300 gp, 75 each)** and **told her about the blind creature**. *Truth: Daggerford clock → 3; translations reach the city in a few days.* She refused the stone cup.
- Nobody they asked knows much about the tower. Biggy wants to build beds and sleep in it.
- **Players' plans:** everyone sleeps, then goes to **the tower**. Then the Reeve (Howling Matza), the charcoal man and the footprints (Bar.D.Sync), the ruins (Allino), the goblin trail and the ruins (Biggy).

> 🎭 **What the players saw.** Serina bought the tablets (about thirty) at 10 gp apiece and is sending them off to be translated: "a few days". She wasn't interested in the stone cup. Nobody seems to know much about the old tower. Evening of Day 2, at the Sundered Cart: coin in pocket, a blind thing under the quarry, goblin tracks at the fold, a maimed quarryman, an unanswered charcoal question, and a Reeve who will want to hear from them.

# 3. Handover: Running Session 2

If you are taking over as DM, this is your checklist. The full book is [Session Two](07-session-two.html).

## 3.1 First ten minutes at the table · DM only

1. Tell the players: **you're level 4.**
2. Read the **"previously on"** box: [Previously on](07-session-two.html#s2-11-previously-on-the-broken-march).
3. Keep **the deadline strip** in view: [the deadlines](07-session-two.html#s2-14-the-deadline-strip-pin-this).
4. Ask: **"Who keeps watch tonight?"** The goblin raid on the fold happens on the Night watch, wherever they are.

## 3.2 The ten things a new DM must not miss · DM only

1. **The fold raid fires tonight** (Day 2 Night): three scouts, food and a count. Nobody dies to it. [Scene 1](07-session-two.html#s2-scene-1-night-at-the-cart-and-the-fold-raid).
2. **Before anyone holds a hammer near the tower, Yorrin says** *"leave it be"*. [Scene 3](07-session-two.html#s2-scene-3-the-tower).
3. **Vaerelin warns them once**, the first time they head for the quarry. Breaking his terms, or any sale, sends the elves to stage 4. [Scene 6](07-session-two.html#s2-scene-6-vaerelins-warning).
4. **The goblin envoy, Nakka, comes to the bridge on Day 3 afternoon**, unarmed. Killing her locks the goblins into war forever. Make it obvious she's a parley. [Scene 8](07-session-two.html#s2-scene-8-the-envoy-at-the-bridge).
5. **Threll's clerk arrives Day 3 morning** with three contracts, each with a catch. [Scene 7](07-session-two.html#s2-scene-7-thrells-clerk-and-serinas-price-list).
6. **The door to the Sentinel is open.** It turns at that door once per watch; you hear it coming 10 minutes ahead. **It cannot be killed.** [The Sentinel ruling](07-session-two.html#s2-the-sentinel-and-the-open-leaf-ruling-for-this-session).
7. **Key A must stay in the socket** or a span of the fence goes dark. [Key A](07-session-two.html#s2-25-the-dungeon-right-now).
8. **Every sale of an Illefarn object** pushes both the elves' and Daggerford's clocks.
9. **Durrow is owed 30 gp**, and he'll ask once. Paying is a kept promise, and the elves notice kept promises.
10. **At the end, run the checklist** and ask each player what their character intends next. [End-of-session checklist](07-session-two.html#s2-7-end-of-session-checklist).
